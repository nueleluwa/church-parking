# Project Introductions

## Version 1: One-Sentence Explanation

Church Parking is a digital parking system that helps churches replace paper tally slips with a simple, secure check-in and exit process for members, guests, and staff.

## Version 2: Short Introduction

Church Parking is a digital parking system for churches that want a simpler and safer way to manage vehicle check-in during services. It is live at churchparking.online and used by Christ Church International. Drivers can check in with a phone or QR code, church staff can verify cars at the exit, and the parking lead can see live counts and follow up on missed check-outs without relying on paper tallies.

## Version 3: Presentation Introduction

Church Parking is designed for churches that need a better way to manage parking during busy services without the hassle of paper tallies. The problem is familiar: handwritten slips can run out, drivers may not have the correct pass at the exit, and church staff are left trying to track vehicles manually while the congregation is moving in and out. This project replaces that process with a digital parking pass, an easy check-in flow, and a clearer operational view for the team on duty.

Drivers can scan a QR code in the car park, check in their vehicle, and keep a pass on their phone. Members can save their cars and check in with two taps, while guests can register a vehicle quickly for a one-off visit. At the exit, attendants can scan the pass or search for the car to verify the plate and release it if the details match. The parking lead can also monitor live counts, follow up on exceptions, and see visits, vehicles, reports and analytics on a phone or a desktop, making the whole process more organised and easier to run during busy service times.

The practical value is straightforward: a smoother driver experience, a stronger and more visible exit check, and less manual work for the church team. It is focused on the real needs of a church parking operation rather than a generic parking system, making it a useful tool for churches that want to keep their service traffic moving without paper-based friction.
