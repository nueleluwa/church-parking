# Parking team guide (one page)

Print this page. Keep a copy at each exit.

## Before you start

1. Sign in at **churchparking.online/staff** (add it to your home screen: it is called Church Parking Team).
2. **Choose the car park you are standing at.** The app remembers it for your shift. Each car park has its own exit.

## Your job at the exit

1. Open the app and tap **Scan pass**. Ask the driver to show their pass.
2. Point the camera at the QR code. The car's details appear.
3. **Look at the car.** Does the plate on the screen match the plate on the car?
4. **If the card says GUEST or NEW CAR:** ask the driver for their name and phone number. Do not read them out first. Check the first name and the last 4 digits on the screen.
5. Everything matches: tap **Release car**.
6. Anything does not match: tap **Does not match**. Stay with the car. **Do not release it.** Call the coordinator. Only a coordinator can clear the hold, and they must give a reason.

## If the car is from a different car park

A yellow warning names both car parks. This is usually a driver who scanned the wrong sign, not a thief. Check the car as normal. If it is the right car and it is still here, tap **Move to this car park**, then release it as usual. Only attendants and team leads can move a car; drivers cannot.

## If the screen shows a warning

| Screen says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **ALREADY RELEASED** | Do not release. Tell the coordinator. Someone may be using an old pass. |
| **OLD PASS** | The pass is from another day. Do not release. Look the car up by plate. |
| **ON HOLD** | A coordinator must clear it. Stay with the car. |
| **Invalid pass** | Scan again. If it still fails, look the car up by plate. |

## No pass, flat battery or no data

1. Tap **Manual lookup** and type the plate (or phone number, or the reference like PK-7Q4M).
2. Ask the driver for their name and phone number. Check them against the screen.
3. Matches: **Release car**. Not found: tap **Add car** to register it, then ask the coordinator.

## Adding a car by hand

Tap **Add car**. Enter the plate and phone number (name is optional) and choose the car park. Give the driver the reference to remember.

## Adding a new member

Under **More**, tap **Add Member**. First type the driver's phone number or plate and tap **Check**, so you do not add someone twice. If nobody is found, enter their full name, Nigerian phone number and plate, then tap **Add member**. The driver can then check in with their phone number. This works only for a Nigerian number; for any other number, use **Add car** instead. The app refuses a number or plate that already belongs to a member, and your name is recorded.

## Members who check in by phone

At some car parks a member checks in with just their phone number and checks out by scanning an **exit sign** on their own phone. Nothing changes for you: they still have a pass, and you can scan it and **Release car** at any time, especially if the member is having any trouble.

- If the member already checked out with the exit sign, the scan card says **The member checked out with the exit sign. Do not release again.**
- If a member says the exit sign did not work, release the car as usual after checking the plate. If the car is on **Does not match**, the exit sign will not let them out: the coordinator clears that, as before.
- A member who lost their check-in on their phone can scan the check-in sign again and confirm their car; that gives their phone a new check-out code. Or you can release the car.

## If the app stops working (paper fallback)

1. Tell the coordinator at once.
2. Use paper tallies for the rest of that service. Write the plate and phone number, and match them at the exit as before.
3. The coordinator writes down the time the app stopped and when it came back.
4. Cars checked in on the app before it stopped can still be released once it is back. Paper-tally cars are not in the app.

## Reminders

- You have your own account. Never share it. Sign out when you finish.
- Phone numbers are hidden except the last 4 digits. Tapping **Show number** is recorded.
- The coordinator's screen shows every **Does not match** straight away.
- If a driver says they cannot check in, send them to the home page: **Check in** (choose the car park) or **Scan a car park sign**. A team lead can also register the car for them with **Add car**.
