# Attendant guide

The short version of what a volunteer needs at the exit. The one-page version is `docs/attendant-guide.md` in the repository.

1. Sign in at `/staff` with your phone number and password, then choose the car park you are standing at.
2. Tap **Scan pass** and point the camera at the driver's pass.
3. Look at the car. Does the plate on the screen match the plate on the car?
4. Everything matches: **Release car**. Anything does not match: **Does not match**, stay with the car and call the coordinator.

| The screen says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **ALREADY RELEASED** | Do not release. Tell the coordinator. |
| **OLD PASS** | The pass is from another day. Look the car up by plate. |
| **ON HOLD** | Only a coordinator can clear it. Stay with the car. |
| **Invalid pass** | Scan again. If it still fails, look the car up by plate. |
| **A different car park** | Check the car; if it is the right one, tap **Move to this car park**, then release. |

**No pass, flat battery or no data:** use **Manual Lookup** by plate, phone or reference. Ask the driver for their name and phone number without reading them out first.

**Phone numbers** show as the last four digits. Tapping **Show number** is recorded.

**If the app stops working:** tell the coordinator at once and use the paper tally for the rest of that service.
