# Conventions

- Plain, short English for non-technical volunteers. Buttons say exactly what they do ("Release car", not "Submit").
- Mobile first. Plates are stored normalised (`PHC234AB`) and shown as `PHC 234 AB`.
- Times are stored in UTC and shown in Africa/Lagos time.
- Fields are `.input` and buttons are `.btn` (44px) on team screens.
- Filter forms use `GetForm` so they do not reload the page. Admin results are toasts; attendant and driver results stay on screen.
- Deleting works one at a time or in bulk, always after a confirmation. Records with history are kept.
- Never put names or phone numbers in URLs, QR codes or logs.
- The church logo is the brand; never replace it with a letter or symbol.
